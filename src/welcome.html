Frequently asked questions

What does CutSmart do?

It automatically edits talking videos. It removes long pauses and filler words, adds subtitles, and can copy the pacing and colour style of a reference video.

Can I fix a cut I don't agree with?

Yes. Use the timeline to review what was cut and apply a timestamp correction. The video is updated without redoing the whole edit.

Can I change how the subtitles look?

Yes. Choose a standard or animated style, then adjust font, size, colour, background and position. You can re-style without re-editing.

Can I combine several clips?

Yes. Edit several clips and combine them into one video with hard cuts or crossfades, or copy the transitions from a reference video.

Which languages are supported?

CutSmart transcribes English, Urdu, Hindi, Arabic, Chinese, French, German, Spanish, Turkish and Persian, using Whisper. Pick the language when you upload, or leave it on auto-detect.

Is there an evaluation of the result?

Yes. Each finished job has a report that summarises the edit, so you can see how much time was cut and how well it matched the reference.
